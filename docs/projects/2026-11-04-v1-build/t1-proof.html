The skill fix

Round 3 found two lines in outcome-brief that let an agent treat you decide as approval. The fix separates handing over decisions from approving the brief.

Before: approval gateDone when they approve, or say to skip the brief.
After: approval gateHand over the brief, ask for approval, then stop: end your turn. Approval is a yes to the brief they have seen, or an explicit skip the brief, just build it. Answers to your questions, you decide or go with your picks are not approval.
Added to the play back stepWhen they leave it to you, take your recommendations, write the brief with those marked as your calls, and let the brief be the playback. They have handed you the decisions, not the approval.

The rules block added to a repository's AGENTS.md got the same line.

What this doesn't prove yet

  • The apply step (B1). The factory was put in place by the runner, not applied by an agent following START.md.
  • Other tools (B4). Only Claude Code with Opus ran. Codex and Cursor come with T4.
  • Small samples. Three runs per arm. The release bar is a stated rate over 3–5 runs per scenario.
  • Hard asks only. T1 tests a vague ask. The skill's small and clear, just do it path isn't tested here.
