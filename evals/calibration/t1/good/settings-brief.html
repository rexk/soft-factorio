People can set the app up the way they like it

Status: draft product brief, awaiting approval

Value

V0. People using the app can adjust how it looks and how it contacts them in one place, so it fits how they work without asking anyone.

  • V1. Fewer support requests about notifications and display.

Behaviour

IDBehaviour
B1A person can open a settings page from any screen.
B2They can choose a light or dark look, and the whole app follows it at once.
B3They can turn email notifications on or off.
B4Their choices are still there after they reload the page on the same device.
