Team leads can take their data with them

Value

V0. A team lead can get all of the team's records out in a form a spreadsheet opens, so leaving or auditing never depends on us.

Behaviour

  • B1. A team lead can ask for an export of every record their team owns.
  • B2. The export opens in common spreadsheet programs with one row per record.
  • B3. People who aren't team leads don't see the export option.
  • B4. An export of a large team arrives within a few minutes, and the lead is told when it is ready.
